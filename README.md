A make.fun entry for **The Router Confesses**: one line in which a home
Wi-Fi router excuses itself for dropping the connection at the climax of a
movie.

> I dropped the Wi-Fi at the climax because after three years of carrying
> your entire life, the least you could do is look at me for once.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes that line to
`/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
